# Tips & Best Practices

## TL;DR
Maximize your medication adherence with smart scheduling, routines, and app features.

## Daily Routine Tips

### Morning Routine
- **Check Home screen** first thing to see today's doses
- Mark morning meds as taken right after breakfast
- Use the **countdown timer** to plan your morning
- Set phone alarm 5 minutes before dose time

### Evening Routine
- Review Stats tab to see today's progress
- Take evening meds at consistent time
- Mark doses before bed
- Check tomorrow's schedule

### Building Habits
- **Link to existing routines**: "After brushing teeth", "With breakfast"
- Take meds at the same time daily for consistency
- Keep medications near where you'll take them (kitchen, bedside)
- Use visual cues (medication box, pill organizer)

## Using App Features Effectively

### Snooze Feature
Best uses for "Snooze 15m":
- Waiting for food to arrive
- Currently driving
- In a meeting or appointment
- Need to finish current task

Don't abuse snooze:
- Limit to 1-2 snoozes per dose
- Eventually mark as Taken or Missed
- Repeated snoozing = schedule might need adjustment

### Undo Button
- Tapped wrong button? Use **Undo** immediately
- Available in snackbar for ~5 seconds
- Also available on the dose card itself
- Prevents need to edit history

### Medication Notes
Add helpful reminders:
- "Take with food"
- "Avoid dairy 2 hours before/after"
- "May cause drowsiness"
- "Take on empty stomach"
- "Drink full glass of water"

### Multiple Times Per Day
Schedule strategically:
- **Morning (7-9am)**: After breakfast
- **Afternoon (12-2pm)**: With lunch
- **Evening (6-8pm)**: With dinner
- **Bedtime (9-11pm)**: Before sleep

## Improving Adherence

### Aim for 80%+
- This is considered good adherence
- Don't expect 100% perfection
- Focus on consistency, not perfection
- Celebrate progress

### Build Streaks
- Start with 2-day streak goal
- Gradually increase to 5 days, then 7 days
- Use streak counter as motivation
- Don't give up if streak breaks - start again

### Identify Patterns
Check Stats tab to find:
- Which days you miss most (weekends?)
- Which times are problematic (morning rush?)
- Which medications you forget often
- Trends over weeks

### Adjust Schedules
If you consistently miss a time:
- **Talk to your doctor** about changing schedule
- Move dose to more convenient time
- Combine with strong daily habit
- Use As Needed frequency if appropriate

## Medication Management

### Adding New Medications
- Add as soon as prescribed
- Double-check dosage and times
- Add doctor's special instructions in notes
- Test notification timing

### Reviewing Library
Monthly review checklist:
- Remove discontinued medications
- Update dosages if changed
- Verify times still work for your schedule
- Update notes with new instructions

### Before Doctor Appointments
- Check Stats tab for weekly adherence
- Note which meds you miss often
- Screenshot stats to show doctor
- Discuss schedule adjustments

## Troubleshooting

### "I forgot to mark a dose"
- Better to mark it late than not at all
- Be honest - mark as Missed if you didn't take it
- Don't retroactively mark as Taken if you forgot
- Accuracy matters more than perfect stats

### "My schedule changed"
- Edit the medication immediately
- Update times to match new routine
- Consider frequency change if needed
- Stats will reflect new schedule going forward

### "I'm missing too many doses"
Common solutions:
- Set multiple alarms on your phone
- Use phone's medication reminder apps alongside this app
- Ask family member to remind you
- Move medications to visible location
- Simplify schedule with doctor's help

## Working with Healthcare Team

### Share Progress
- Show Stats screen to your doctor
- Discuss patterns you've noticed
- Be honest about missed doses
- Ask for help with difficult schedules

### Questions to Ask
- "Can I take all morning meds at once?"
- "Can this move to a more convenient time?"
- "Do I really need this four times daily?"
- "Can I use a combination pill instead?"

## Privacy & Data

### Local Storage
- All data stays on your device
- No account needed
- No cloud backup (yet)
- Uninstalling = data loss

### Backing Up
Currently no built-in backup:
- Write down medications and schedules
- Take screenshots of library
- Keep original prescriptions

## Getting Help

### App Issues
- Restart app if buttons don't respond
- Check device storage if app is slow
- Update to latest version
- Report bugs to developer

### Medical Questions
- Always consult your doctor or pharmacist
- App is a tracking tool, not medical advice
- Don't change medications without doctor approval
- Emergency? Call 911, don't rely on app

## Final Tips

### Keep It Simple
- Start with just your most important medications
- Add others gradually
- Don't overcomplicate notes
- Trust the simple workflow

### Be Consistent
- Mark doses at the same time you take them
- Check app daily
- Review stats weekly
- Adjust as needed monthly

### Stay Motivated
- Celebrate streaks
- Share progress with family
- Notice health improvements
- Remember why adherence matters
