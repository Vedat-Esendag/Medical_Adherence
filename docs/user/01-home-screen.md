# Home Screen - Daily Dose Tracking

## TL;DR
Your main hub for today's medication schedule with live countdown, quick action buttons, and weekly stats at a glance.

## Header Card

### Next Dose Countdown
- **Real-time countdown** showing time until your next scheduled dose
- Displays medication name, dosage, and scheduled time
- Example: "3h 24m" until "Amlodipine 5mg at 19:00"

### Take Now Button
- Appears when you're within the dose window
- Quick one-tap to mark the upcoming dose as taken
- Saves time when dose is due

### Weekly Summary
Two key metrics displayed prominently:
- **This Week**: Current week's adherence percentage (e.g., "83%")
- **Streak**: Consecutive days of 100% adherence (e.g., "5 days")

### All Doses Complete
When you've taken all doses for the day:
- Large checkmark (✓)
- "All doses completed!" message
- Motivating confirmation

## Today's Doses List

### Dose Cards
Each medication shows as a card with:
- Pill icon (💊)
- Medication name (e.g., "Amlodipine")
- Dosage and time (e.g., "5 mg at 07:00")
- Three action buttons

### Action Buttons

**Taken** (Primary Blue Button)
- Mark dose as successfully taken
- Collapses card to show "✓ Taken" chip
- Most common action, easiest to tap

**Missed** (Outlined Button)
- Mark dose as missed
- Shows "Missed" chip in gray
- No judgment, just tracking

**Snooze 15m** (Tonal Button)
- Postpone the reminder for 15 minutes
- Helpful when you're busy
- Reschedules just this one dose

### After Marking a Dose
- Card collapses to save space
- Shows status chip (✓ Taken or Missed)
- **Undo button** appears on the right
- Snackbar notification with app-wide Undo option

### Card Menu (⋮)
Tap the three dots for:
- **Edit**: Modify medication details
- **Delete**: Remove medication (with confirmation)

## FAB (Floating Action Button)
- Blue **+** button in bottom right
- Tap to add a new medication
- Always accessible from Home screen

## Empty State
When you haven't added medications yet:
- Friendly message: "No medications yet"
- Instruction to tap + button
- Direct "Add Medication" button for convenience

## Tips
- Mark doses as you take them for accurate tracking
- Use Undo if you tap the wrong button
- Snooze is great for meals - take meds after eating
- The app won't judge you for missed doses
