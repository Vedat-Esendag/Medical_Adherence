# Medications Library - Managing Your Medications

## TL;DR
View, add, edit, and delete all your medications with complete schedule details and notes.

## Medications List

### Library View
- Shows all medications with full details
- Count displayed in top bar: "My Medications (5)"
- Each card shows complete schedule, not just next dose

### Medication Card Details
- **Pill icon** (💊) for visual recognition
- **Name**: Medication name (e.g., "Metoprolol")
- **Dosage**: Strength (e.g., "50 mg")
- **Schedule section**:
  - Frequency type (Daily, Specific Days, etc.)
  - All scheduled times (e.g., "Daily at 07:00", "Daily at 19:00")
- **Notes**: Optional special instructions (italicized)

### Example Card
```
💊 Amlodipine
5 mg

Schedule:
• Daily at 07:00
• Daily at 19:00

Take with food
```

## Adding a Medication

### Step-by-Step
1. Tap the **+ FAB** button (bottom right)
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Medication Name**: e.g., "Amlodipine"
   - **Dosage**: e.g., "5 mg"
   - **Frequency**: Choose from dropdown
   - **Times**: Add one or more times
   - **Notes** (optional): e.g., "Take with food"
3. Tap **Save** button at bottom

### Frequency Options
- **Daily**: Every day at the specified times
- **Specific Days**: Choose which days (Mon, Tue, Wed, etc.)
- **Every X Days**: Repeats every N days
- **Weekly**: Once per week on chosen day
- **As Needed**: For PRN medications

### Multiple Times Per Day
- Tap "Add Time" to schedule multiple doses
- Example: Morning (07:00) and evening (19:00)
- Each time shows in the library with full details

## Editing a Medication

### How to Edit
1. Find the medication in the library
2. Tap the **⋮** menu icon
3. Select **Edit**
4. Make your changes
5. Tap **Save**

### What You Can Change
- Name and dosage
- Schedule times
- Frequency pattern
- Notes

## Deleting a Medication

### Delete Process
1. Tap the **⋮** menu icon on the medication card
2. Select **Delete**
3. Confirm in the dialog: "This will permanently remove this medication and all its history"
4. Tap **Delete** to confirm or **Cancel** to abort

### Warning
Deletion is permanent and removes:
- The medication entry
- All dose history
- All statistics related to this medication

Cannot be undone.

## Validation

### Required Fields
- Medication name cannot be empty
- Dosage cannot be empty
- At least one time must be specified

### Save Button
- Disabled (grayed out) until form is valid
- Prevents saving incomplete data
- Shows error messages under invalid fields

## Empty State
When you haven't added medications:
- Large pill emoji (💊)
- "No medications yet" message
- "Tap the + button to add your first medication"

## Tips
- Add notes for reminders like "Take with food" or "Avoid dairy"
- Use specific days for medications like "Monday, Wednesday, Friday"
- Review your library periodically to ensure schedules are current
- Delete discontinued medications to keep your list clean
